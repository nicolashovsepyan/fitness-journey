Where we are

Core and Push are curated. The rest is not.

"Curated" means a movement has a level, a difficulty, and a place in a progression chain. Without those three the generator cannot pick a level-appropriate variant, which is the whole mechanism behind scaling a session.

The new axis you asked for

Three disciplines, and skills sit inside one of them

Every movement now carries a discipline. It is a separate question from the mechanical pattern — a hinge can be a barbell deadlift or a kettlebell swing, and those belong in different worlds even though the body does the same thing.

The shape of it

What we have, by category

Twenty-eight fundamentals across seven categories. Each carries a bodyweight ladder and, where one honestly exists, a gym lane. The bar shows how many ladders sit in each category — the gaps are as informative as the totals.

The shape you asked for

Every fundamental is a ladder, not an entry

One anchor — the movement we are getting you to. Regressions to its left so somebody just starting can enter today. Progressions to its right so nobody runs out. Beneath each one, the gym lane: the loaded version of the same job, shorter on purpose because load is the progression. Five have no honest equivalent and say so.
