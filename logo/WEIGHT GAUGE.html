Fitness Journey — the weight gauge

The mark's dumbbell, loaded.

A weight selector built out of the logo rather than beside it. Five pounds to a hundred and twenty is a 24× range — far too much for thickness alone, which is why the plates gain diameter and thickness together. A plate's mass is its volume, so scaling both means weight rises with the cube of the size: 24× the weight is only 2.88× the plate. Physically true, and it fits in a card.

The whole range at once

Every rung the selector will stop on. The step between rungs is what decides whether this reads as a gauge or as decoration.

5 → 120+

The cube root compresses the top: 5→10 lb is a bigger visual step than 110→120 lb, because doubling five pounds is a bigger change than adding nine percent. That is honest, and it is also why the plus matters — past 120 the graphic stops growing and says so, instead of pretending to keep scaling.
