Fitness Journey — the mark

An inverted neon triangle, with a dumbbell centred in it.

Drawn from an 80s album cover. The outline is an open polyline with a dead segment in the top-left, the way a real neon tube has one. Everything you see hangs off a single 4400 ms rep — the light finishes its lap along the top edge at the exact instant the glow behind the dumbbell swells, and the outline thumps once, there, and nowhere else.

As a stamp

Small sizes are where a mark fails. Below about 20 px the dumbbell's outer plates close up and the triangle keeps reading — which decides which half does the marking.
