Fitness Journey — the mark

An inverted neon triangle, with a dumbbell centred in it.

Drawn from an 80s album cover. The outline is an open polyline with a dead segment in the top-left, the way a real neon tube has one. No words are part of it. Everything hangs off a single 4400 ms rep: the tube is lit, one short light runs the perimeter, and the mark breathes. Nothing pulses, flashes or thumps — it is a neon sign, and a neon sign is simply on.

As a stamp

Small sizes are where a mark fails. Below about 20 px the dumbbell's outer plates close up and the triangle keeps reading — which decides which half does the marking.

Selection

The triangle inks in when a row is chosen. It is the mark doing a job the app already needs done, rather than a logo parked in a corner.

The second logo

The name, set as its own lockup — used where the mark alone wouldn't identify anything. The line under it is the open question: it has to carry intent, not describe a product.

Home screen

Outline weight against the plates

The hero's own proportion works out at 2.86 px — measured against the outer plate, the widest plate and the bar, it gives the same answer all three ways. That is too thin for a tile drawn at 60 px, so 4.5 is the compromise in use. The number under each is its ratio to the outermost plate; the hero's is 0.36.
