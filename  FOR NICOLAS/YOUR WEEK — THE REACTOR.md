# Screen 25 — "Your week", redesigned

> *"I just don't know how we can do it for them to be able to gauge it
> properly. I wouldn't know how to answer how many points for my sleep."*

**That is the whole problem, and it is not a wording problem.** Nobody can
answer an abstract share. What people *can* do is recognise themselves in a
shape, and then argue with it.

So: **never ask for a number. Offer a shape, then let them bend it — and show
every channel in real life, not in points.**

---

## The move that makes it answerable

Your sleep example is the proof. "How many points is your sleep" is
unanswerable. **"7 hours a night, one easy day"** is answerable instantly,
because it is a fact about your life rather than a rating of it.

So every channel is labelled with what its share *means*, and the label
changes as you drag:

| Channel | Low end | High end |
|---|---|---|
| **WORK** | 35-hour weeks | 55-hour weeks, evenings gone |
| **PEOPLE** | Weekends only | Most evenings are theirs |
| **RECOVERY** | Under 6 hours, no easy days | 8 hours, a real rest day |
| **TRAINING** | 2 sessions, whatever is left | 5 sessions, planned first |

You are not rating anything. You are reading four sentences about your life
and moving the one that is wrong.

**It also kills screen 24.** Sleep is not a separate question any more — it is
what the Recovery channel says.

---

## Page 1 — THE REACTOR

Four vertical neon columns, like reactor rods or an equaliser. A glowing core
between them. Retro readout above.

**They never start from zero.** Three presets, and picking one lights the rods:

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| **STEADY** | Everything gets its share. Nothing dominates. | The honest default |
| **SURGE** | Training is the priority right now. Other things flex. | Someone with a window |
| **HOLDING** | Life is full. Training goes in the gaps. | Most people, most of the time |

Then they drag any rod. **The others move to compensate** — the budget is
fixed, so it is a real trade-off, which is exactly Galpin's point. Take from
Recovery to feed Training and you watch Recovery fall.

**The core is the honesty meter.** It glows steady jade when the mix is
sustainable. Push Training up while Recovery drops and it shifts amber, then
red, and pulses harder — the same ember-and-spark language as the hero. No
warning text, no scolding. You can see it is unstable.

That is the whole screen. Pick a shape, move one or two rods, watch the core.
**Ten seconds if you agree with the preset.**

### Why four channels and not ten points

Ten points across four areas is arithmetic. Four rods is a picture. And the
number does not have to be 10 — the rods are proportions of a week, so the
maths is invisible and the trade-off is not.

---

## The transition — READING YOUR WEEK

Short. The rods settle, the core steadies, and three lines type out as they
are computed:

> Football, twice a week — counted.
> Recovery at 7 hours — that holds 4 hard sessions.
> Training at a third of your week — realistic.

**This is where screens 21 and 22 finally do something.** Right now "anything
else in your week" and "how much of it" are collected and never mentioned
again. Here they are visibly subtracted before anything is proposed, which is
the moment the person believes we were listening.

---

## Page 2 — THE PROPOSAL

Big retro readout, stated as a conclusion rather than a question:

> ## 4 days · 45 minutes
> Built from your rods, minus the football.

Two steppers underneath to change either. **The line under it updates as they
push**, and it pushes back honestly:

- Go to 6 days → *"6 days at that recovery is where people stall. I would hold
  you at 5."*
- Drop to 2 days → *"2 is enough to get stronger. It is not enough to get
  stronger fast — that is a fine trade."*
- Push session length past what the rods allow → *"That is 30 minutes more a
  week than your rods have room for. Something has to give."*

They can override anything. **We just never pretend the trade is free.**
