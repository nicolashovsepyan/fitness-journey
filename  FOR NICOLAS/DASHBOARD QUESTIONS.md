# Questions parked for the dashboard

Five questions came out of the survey. **None are deleted** — they are asked
once someone is inside, in a "finish your profile" panel that earns a little
progress each time. The survey stays about the program; these are about how we
speak to them afterwards.

Why they moved: none of them changes what gets programmed on day one, and the
survey has to justify every screen.

| 12 | **Which numbers do you look at?** | See below — the most interesting of the five. |

## The one worth keeping — "which numbers do you look at?"

This is not a settings question. **What someone chooses to watch says what
keeps them going**, and the two are not the same person:

- Watching **sleep and recovery** → they respond to being told to back off.
  Push them and they quit.
- Watching **weight and calories** → outcome-driven. They need the scale to
  move or they lose faith, so early wins have to be visible.
- Watching **load, volume, PRs** → process-driven. They will keep going
  through a plateau if the numbers are still climbing.
- Watching **steps and streaks** → consistency-driven. Never break the chain
  beats any programming decision we make.
- Watching **nothing** → the honest majority. Do not build a dashboard that
  assumes they care about a chart.

**Same program, different app.** It changes which number goes at the top of
their dashboard, what the weekly message says, and what we push when they
stall. Worth asking properly once they are in, rather than as screen 12 of a
survey where it changes nothing yet.

## The check-in needs designing first

"Pick the day we talk" promised a conversation the app cannot have. What it
was pointing at is a real feature and a good one:

> Once a week, a short set of questions. What did you do. How did each session
> feel. Is that more or less than last week. Anything hurting. Then one line
> back that means something.

That is its own format — closer to the session runner than to a survey — and
it belongs in the same pass as the dashboard. Asking someone to pick a day for
it before it exists is asking them to commit to nothing.

**Say when you want to take this on and I will design it properly.**
