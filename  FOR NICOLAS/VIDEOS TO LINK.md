## Why this is a form and not a finished job

I can find a video for every one of these in a few minutes. What I cannot do is judge
whether it demonstrates the movement the way YOU coach it — and a fundamental with a
demo that contradicts your own cue is worse than a fundamental with no demo, because
the person believes the video.

So: paste a URL after each `=`, leave the rest blank, and run
`node tools/link-videos.mjs`. Anything still blank stays blank.

Or tell me which channels you trust and I will fill it in and you review the list.
